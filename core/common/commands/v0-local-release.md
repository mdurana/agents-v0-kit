## Prerequisite & Resume
1. If `<feature>` or `<id>` was not given, follow the naming rule in `workflow-orchestration.mdc` Parallel Features. 3. `guard-local-release.sh check <feature> [--maintain]` verifies the `<feature>` slug, confirms the story is not already released (suggesting `/v0-maintain`, `/v0-triage`, or `/v0-intake` with a new feature slug if already released, or `/v0-verify <id>` for an emergency maintenance note that still owes Verify), and verifies `"verifyPassed": true` and `"verify"` in `approvedPhases` backed by the user's external verify approval record (`sh .agents-v0/scripts/guard-common.sh approve verify <feature>`) and committed Verify gate (or `tier: "emergency"` maintenance note with rollback plan and `Follow-up verify due`). ## Collision
